BICTool 1.0.0 - image-quality assessment of head CT
===================================================

Start
  Double-click BICTool.exe. No installation and no administrator rights are needed.
  Keep the whole folder together: BICTool.exe needs the folders _internal and models.

  Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time (the program is not
  signed). Click "More info" and then "Run anyway".

Manual
  English and German user manuals: folder "manual", or Help > User manual in the program.

Requirements
  Windows 10 (1809 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit; 8 GB RAM recommended;
  about 3 GB free disk space.

Where BICTool keeps things
  Log of the last session:  %LOCALAPPDATA%\BICTool\logs\bictool.log
  Settings (window, text size, last export folder): %APPDATA%\BfS\BICTool.ini

Remove
  Delete the BICTool folder.
