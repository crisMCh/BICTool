# Model card: head-CT study-level quality model

Generated by `python -m bictool.application.train_classifier fit-study` on 2026-10-06. Do not edit by hand - rerun the command. Background, analysis and decisions: `head_ct_classifier.md`; feature measurement: `physical_qa_recipe.md`.

## 1. Model details

* **Version:** head-ct-1
* **File:** `source/bictool/domain/results/_head_ct_model.npz`; evaluated by `domain/results/head_ct_verdict.py` (NumPy only).
* **Type:** logistic regression (L2, C = 1, class-balanced) on standardised features.
* **Input:** for one study, the median over its falx (dural folds) slices of 5 features from the physical QA recipe: mtf_area (MTF area (sharpness)), nps_homo (Noise in homogeneous tissue (ROI2 NPS)), nps_struct (Noise at the falx (ROI1 NPS)), var_struct (Variance at the falx (ROI1)), ratio_lf_hf (PS/MTF ratio (LF/HF)).
* **Output:** the probability that the study is rated acceptable, with a 95 % interval; verdict **Acceptable** (whole interval > 0.5), **Not acceptable** (whole interval < 0.5) or **Uncertain**; features outside the range of the good training studies; a provisional artefact indicator; outlier slices; a falx/septum plausibility check.
* **Uncertainty:** 1000 refits on bootstrap resamples of the training studies (stratified by class, seed 2026), each paired with a resample of the study's slices; the interval is the 2.5-97.5 percentile of the resulting probabilities.

## 2. Intended use

* Decision support for image-quality QA of routine adult head CT, soft-tissue head kernels, reconstructed in axial ~4 mm slices, measured with the physical QA recipe in BICTool.
* Not for: other body regions, sharp/bone kernels, the legacy (octave) recipe, single slices, or any diagnostic purpose. The verdict replaces no radiologist.
* No verdict with fewer than 5 usable falx slices; no artefact indicator with fewer than 10.

## 6. Limitations

* One rater; no inter-reader variability known; rating criteria not documented.
* Small sample: exact intervals above are wide, and the design was chosen after looking at the same training studies (`head_ct_classifier.md` 5.5) - cross-validation is optimistic.
* Scanner-rating confounding; only one good Canon study.
* Only soft-tissue head kernels; scope of the physical recipe.
* The artefact indicator rests on 2 artefact studies; the test set has none.
* Dose was not used (decision D2).
* The model does not see metal streaks well (ROI2 avoids them). In the app a rule outside the model sets the verdict to Not assessable when metal streaks reach the brain (`head_ct_classifier.md` 6.6); the model's own result is kept in the details.
